## 描述

Tasks could lose progress ownership after restart, repeat completed work, or reuse evidence under different inputs. This PR persists immutable task/operation bindings, makes recovery explicit and preserves the evidence needed to distinguish reusable results from unknown outcomes. Offline subtitle tasks now also retain their selected language through save, restart and batch reconstruction.

### Resulting behavior

- Queue operations bind immutable inputs across acquisition, subtitles, PPT/subtitle batches and direct-SRT summaries. Prepared outputs and completion receipts are reusable; UNKNOWN provider outcomes never replay automatically. Task-center refreshes serialize expensive snapshots and discard stale UI responses.
- Media readiness is independently SHA-verified rather than inferred from catalog identifiers. Station subtitles bind URL/page/SRT. Only explicit NO_SUBTITLE enters ASR; auth/rate/cancel/timeout classes remain actionable and stop affected batches without automatic retry.
- Cookie generations fence stale requests and login/logout races; browser credential snapshots use shell acknowledgement. Controlled persistence failures preserve the previous session state.
- Summary configuration, request attempts, accepted points, publication plans and final-file receipts retain bounded evidence. Read-only audits distinguish unavailable, incomplete, invalid and complete snapshots. Saved references bind the exact subtitle text/hash and cannot silently become video playback claims.
- Frame selection and visual extraction persist selected-only manifests and factual output receipts. Changed-output recovery preserves the original. Legacy evidence remains unknown where no historical proof exists; submitted/cited coverage is not invented.
- Managed-media transport verifies one held source handle and streamed blocks, bounds ownership/ranges/expiry, and supports opt-in local retention and explicit fenced release. Summary-from-retained inputs bind the saved authority and request. This does not complete the summary-bound player UI.
- Native ASR cue endpoints stay within real speech ranges, and cancellation/deadline causes survive Go/native dispatch. Prepared ASR v2 records source/SRT and observed adapter/language facts; loaded model identity remains not-attested.
- The new subtitle language selector offers Chinese (default), English and auto for local, remote and batch requests. Language is immutable within an operation; omission during recovery uses the saved binding, explicit conflicts fail, and a changed selection creates a new operation. Legacy Chinese identity bytes remain compatible. Non-Chinese saved reuse requires matching V2 ASR provenance or independently verified station-subtitle evidence. Station subtitles are not forced through ASR.
- Main/preload keep a bounded safe error envelope and trusted sender checks. Confirmed long-running media/model actions use the existing long job budget rather than the short shell default.
